This repository is a community preservation mirror of the **Infidex 176 V** open source panoramic film camera.

- **Original author:** Denis Aminev (Time to Waste)
- **Original site:** timetowaste.ru (going offline 21 March 2027)

The author granted permission to redistribute the project files on any platform:

> Feel free to distribute files of the project on any other website or a platform.

`Infidex 176 V manual.pdf` is included unchanged.

If you fork or re-mirror this, keep this notice intact.
